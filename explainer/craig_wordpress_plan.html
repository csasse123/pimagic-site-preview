Why he wants it this way

  • He can change it himself. A new price, a new image, a fixed typo: done in the WordPress editor in a minute, without waiting for a new plugin.
  • WordPress already runs the rest. The shop, the ordering page, licences, tutorials and the menu all live in WordPress. Keeping the home page there too means one system.
  • It is how he already works. His mock-up (page 642) is built exactly this way, with placeholders where our pieces go.

What to watch on his mock-up today

  • The film cover says “PixInsight. Made simple.” That is the old message you ruled out; the new cover says “Astrophotography. Made simple.”
  • “Three clicks” — we agreed on two: select a folder, press Start.
  • “For Windows and Mac” — our pages say Mac, Windows and Linux. One of them needs to change.
  • “Photohsop” — a typo in the AstroMagic section.
  • The white theme can come back wherever a plain WordPress block is added without the dark background, as it did on his first version.

My recommendation
