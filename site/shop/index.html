// questions

Before you buy

Do I need to know PixInsight?

No. PixInsight is the engine under the hood — PiMagic runs it for you. Select a folder, press Start. (Know PixInsight already? Expert mode opens every setting.)

What do I need?

PixInsight 1.9.5 on Mac or Windows. RC-Astro's BlurXTerminator, NoiseXTerminator and StarXTerminator are used when installed. Photoshop only for AstroMagic — PiMagic's TIFF and JPEG results open in just about any image editor.

The download or license activation is blocked.

By far the most common cause is a firewall or antivirus program. Pause both briefly to complete the installation.

Do I need my own stacking software?

No. Select the folder with your lights, flats, darks and bias — it calibrates and stacks for you. You can also keep a calibration library and let PiMagic pick the matching masters.

Which cameras work?

Mono and one-shot-color astro cameras, DSLRs and mirrorless cameras, smart telescopes and fisheye lenses — deep sky, nightscapes and mosaics.
