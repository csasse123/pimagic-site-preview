// how it works

Three steps. That's the whole workflow.

01

Point it at a folder

Lights, flats, darks and bias — PiMagic reads every file, sorts it by itself and understands what you shot: camera, filters, exposures, target.

02

Smart or Expert

Smart mode decides everything from your data. Expert mode opens every control. One switch at the top of the window.

03

Press Start

Calibrated, stacked and finished — all by itself. Here a real 15-minute run, sped up. Then the image is yours to shape.

Smart mode · decides for you

  • Reads your data and picks the right route: deep sky, nightscape, mosaic, narrowband or broadband
  • Matches calibration frames — from the folder or your calibration library, same camera, nearest in time
  • Recognises every filter and builds the right colour images (RGB, LRGB, SHO, HOO, dual-band)
  • Ideal if you are new to PixInsight — and a huge time-saver if you are not

Expert mode · you take control

  • Every stage and parameter is open: calibration, stacking, gradients, colour, stretching, star handling
  • Edit the filter mapping, choose palettes, blend options and mosaic projection
  • Run the same data with different settings and compare — without repeating any step by hand
  • "Reset to Recommended" brings back Smart's choices at any time

// questions

Good to know.

What do I need?

PixInsight 1.9.5 on Mac, Windows or Linux. RC-Astro's BlurXTerminator, NoiseXTerminator and StarXTerminator are used when installed (on Mac also the free RC-Astro command-line tool). Photoshop is needed only for AstroMagic.

How is it installed?

PiMagic installs through the PixInsight repository; the download email has the details and an installation guide. If a download or licence activation is blocked, your firewall or antivirus is the usual cause — pause it briefly to complete the installation.

Which cameras and filters work?

Mono and one-shot-colour astro cameras, DSLRs and mirrorless cameras, smart telescopes and fisheye lenses; L, R, G, B, Hα, OIII, SII and dual-band filters.

Do I lose control?

No — that is the point. PiMagic takes over the routine; the creative decisions stay yours. Smart mode decides the processing for you, Expert mode opens every setting, and the results are clean layered files you finish as you like.

How do I receive my order?

By email, with the download and installation information and your receipt. Nothing arrived? Check your spam folder.
