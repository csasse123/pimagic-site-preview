// questions

Good to know

Do I need to know PixInsight?

No. PixInsight is the engine under the hood — PiMagic runs it for you. Select a folder, press Start. (Know PixInsight already? Expert mode opens every setting.)

What do I need?

PixInsight 1.9.5 on Mac or Windows. RC-Astro's BlurXTerminator, NoiseXTerminator and StarXTerminator are used when installed (on Mac also the free RC-Astro command-line tool). Photoshop is needed only for AstroMagic.

How is it installed?

PiMagic installs through the PixInsight repository; the download email has the details and an installation guide. If a download or license activation is blocked, your firewall or antivirus is the usual cause — pause it briefly to complete the installation.

Which cameras and filters work?

Mono and one-shot-color astro cameras, DSLRs and mirrorless cameras, smart telescopes and fisheye lenses; L, R, G, B, Hα, OIII, SII and dual-band filters.

Do I lose control?

No — that is the point. PiMagic takes over the routine; the creative decisions stay yours. Smart mode decides the processing for you, Expert mode opens every setting, and the results are clean layered files you finish as you like.

How do I receive my order?

By email, with the download and installation information and your receipt. Nothing arrived? Check your spam folder.
